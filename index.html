INTRODUCTION

Although it may seem scary or not doable, this guide will guide you through the whole process of building a Gaming PC and show that it isn't as difficult as it seems. Anyone can do it with the proper knowledge, it is almost like building Lego. As well, In the world of PC gaming, building your own PC is without a doubt the best way to enjoy it. It allows you to have full control on everything: the parts that go inside, the way it looks, the software that you install. It is completely up to you. It is also the most cost efficient and it truly ensures that you get the most amount of performance per dollar. In addition, PCs can be designed to look or perform in unimaginable ways, from a simple mini PC all the way to a custom water-cooled monster. The world of PC building is endless in possibilities, and here you will find your guide through the whole process to building your own, unique gaming system.
